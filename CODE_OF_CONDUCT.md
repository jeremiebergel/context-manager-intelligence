# Code of conduct

CMI is a small project; this is the short version of how we work together.

- Be kind and assume good faith. Disagree with ideas, never with people.
- Help newcomers: a first question or a first PR deserves a real answer.
- Back claims with evidence (a test, a benchmark, a log). This applies to maintainers too.
- No harassment, insults, sexual content, or personal attacks, in any project space
  (issues, pull requests, Discussions, and any future chat).
- Do not post other people's private data, credentials or transcripts.

**Reporting.** Contact the maintainer privately: open a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/jeremiebergel/context-manager-intelligence/security/advisories/new)
with "Conduct" in the title, or use GitHub's "Report content" on the comment. Reports are handled
confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove content, and warn, temporarily block or ban
participants who break these rules.

Adapted in spirit from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
